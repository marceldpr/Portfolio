TruScope

Bringing AI into a complex
construction product.

Bringing AR, drawing management, and AI together in one construction product. A redesigned experience that improved adoption and helped teams turn complex project documents into tasks and inspections.

I started by separating visible UI problems from deeper adoption friction. As the product changed, I helped turn a business pivot into a coherent product direction.

The problem.

TruScope’s AR technology worked, but friction in core workflows and navigation held back adoption. As the business expanded into sheet management and inspections, the challenge grew: connecting these new capabilities with AR in a coherent, intuitive experience across iPhone, iPad, and web.

The insight.

Familiar patterns made the product easier to learn, but familiarity alone wasn't enough to convince users to switch from their existing tools. To compete in a new market, we needed to go beyond usability and give construction teams a compelling reason to change how they worked.

The solution.

I mapped complex, paperwork-heavy workflows to uncover friction and opportunities for automation. I brought key moments of value forward so users could experience the benefits sooner, while building on familiar patterns from competing tools—preserving what worked and improving what didn't.

The adoption challenge.

TruScope could overlay BIM models onto the real world, but getting users to experience that value was another story.

Friction in onboarding, navigation, and everyday field tasks made the product harder to use than it needed to be. Despite the working AR technology, adoption remained low.

Beyond augmented reality.

Midway through the redesign, the business expanded beyond AR into sheet management and inspections.

What began as an effort to improve usability became a broader product design challenge: connecting AR, documentation, and field workflows across iPhone, iPad, and web without creating a fragmented experience.

The previous homepage acted primarily as a menu, even though field work revolves around tasks, issues, inspections, and deadlines happening at the same time.

I redesigned it around what needs the user’s attention now. The new For You experience surfaces upcoming deadlines, assigned work, and relevant issues first, turning the homepage into a personalized starting point for the day.

01 / Barrier

Hundreds of sheets.
Constant revisions.

Construction projects require uploading hundreds of sheets across different trades, each with multiple revisions that need to be organized and tracked.

Keep

Automatic metadata extraction and sheet version management.

Improve

Finding and correcting extraction errors without excessive scrolling.

Show it once. Automate the rest.

I designed a guided setup that lets users identify where sheet information appears just once. TruScope reuses that location across the remaining sheets and future uploads, with clear instructions for newcomers that experienced users can dismiss.

Verify once. Apply to hundreds.

TruScope instantly previews the extracted information, allowing users to correct the selection before processing hundreds of sheets. Once confirmed, the location of each field is saved and reused for future uploads, eliminating the need to repeat the setup.

Human control

Users can review and edit extracted values, redraw the area, or bulk-rescan pages when something is wrong.

02 / Barrier

Thousands of pages.
Countless requirements.

Construction project manuals can span thousands of pages of contractually binding requirements, covering everything from vendors and materials to the specific tasks and inspections required on-site. Every requirement needs to be identified, organized, and tracked.

Turning documentation into action.

01 /

Reuse existing work

Extract vendors, tasks, and inspections directly from project manuals instead of asking users to recreate them manually.

02 /

Review before committing

Let users review extracted information, with clear progress and system status, before adding anything to their project.

03 /

Keep humans in control

Make steps optional and let users resolve ambiguous results, rather than relying on AI guesses or blocking their progress.

Vendor information. Already organized.

TruScope automatically extracts vendor information from the project manual, separates existing platform users from new vendors, and categorizes them as single-source, multi-source, or preferred suppliers based on project requirements.

The right tasks. The right vendors.

TruScope automatically matches tasks extracted from the project manual to vendors based on their trade and sourcing type. Instead of reading specifications, cross-referencing vendor lists, and assigning tasks one by one, teams get a pre-populated task list with responsibilities already mapped according to project requirements.

Assigned. Invited. Ready to work.

TruScope cross-references assigned vendors with those identified in the first step, automatically determining who needs an invitation and sending it. This eliminates manual onboarding, avoids duplicate invitations, and helps ensure the right people have access to their assigned work from the start.
